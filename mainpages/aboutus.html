About Us

How It Started


Our story first starts in late 2025, when the four of us had just graduated from our university with a bachelor's degree after three long years of studying. After graduating, we decided to take some time off and pursue some of our hobbies before starting our work life.


Since we all had anime as a shared interest and had always dreamt of creating our own website to show off to the world, we decided to make use of our free time and put our web development knowledge to good use. So, we made a plan to create our very own website dedicated to everything and anything anime related, which also included cosplays.


Through months and months of hard work and pure dedication, we realised our dream and successfully created a full-fledged website that has now managed to bring together anime fans from all around the world, creating a community full of people that are incredibly passionate and welcoming. We are incredibly proud of what we've achieved and are grateful for everyone who supported us along the way.

Our Goals


These are the goals we hope to achieve with our website:

1. To provide the latest news and updates regarding anything anime.

2. To introduce people to the world of anime.

3. To promote anime as an entertainment medium.

4. To promote cosplay as a hobby.

5. To drive interaction within the anime community.
